VEYRA

An AI front desk for dental and aesthetics clinics

Patients message your clinic on WhatsApp or Instagram. Veyra answers in their language, books the appointment, and reminds them before they come. Your team steps in whenever it matters.

What it does

Answers questions

Opening hours, services and prices, taken only from the clinic's own information.

Books appointments

Checks real availability, then books, reschedules or cancels.

Sends reminders

Automatic WhatsApp reminders before each appointment.

Hands over to staff

Anything medical, urgent or unclear goes straight to a person.

Arabic and English

Replies in natural Saudi Arabic or in English, following the patient's language.

Built with care for patient data

The assistant does not give medical advice and collects only a name, a service and a time. Read our privacy policy.
